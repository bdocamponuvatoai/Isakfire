## Claims on the live site that we left off

Each is a question for the client. Don't add any of them without the owner's confirmation.

| Claim on isakfire.com                                                                                      | Why it's off                                                                     | Ask the client                                                                                             |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| "Certified, code-compliant fire protection systems" and "One Certified Team"                               | Certification of whom, by which body?                                            | Which certifications do the company and technicians hold (NICET levels, state licences)? Can we name them? |
| "NICET-certified technicians" (sprinklers)                                                                 | Applies to every technician?                                                     | How many technicians hold NICET, and at which levels?                                                      |
| "Nationwide and in Canada"                                                                                 | The site lists six offices in five states and Ontario                            | Do you take work outside those regions? Where?                                                             |
| "We respond within 1 business day"                                                                         | A promise customers hold you to                                                  | Is a one-business-day response guaranteed? For quotes, service, or both?                                   |
| "Written proposal typically within 2–3 business days"                                                      | Timing promise                                                                   | Is that still accurate, and can we publish it?                                                             |
| "Schedules provided upfront, and we consistently meet them"                                                | Unverifiable performance claim                                                   | Can you share an example or a figure we can stand behind?                                                  |
| Annual agreements with "priority scheduling" and "discounted labor rates"                                  | Commercial terms                                                                 | Are those terms current and standard for every agreement?                                                  |
| Specific inspection intervals (quarterly, annual, 5-year; semi-annual kitchen) and "30–60 days to correct" | Varies by code edition and jurisdiction                                          | Happy for the site to stay general and direct people to the team?                                          |
| "Operating with an outdated design voids compliance and insurance coverage"                                | Legal and insurance claim                                                        | Do you want any insurance wording, reviewed by counsel?                                                    |
| "Since 2014" and "EST. 2014"                                                                               | Years in business. It's in the logo, but not repeated in copy                    | Confirm the founding year so it can appear in copy.                                                        |
| Brands (Notifier, Simplex, Honeywell, Ansul, Amerex)                                                       | May imply dealer or authorised-installer status                                  | Are you an authorised dealer or installer for any brand?                                                   |
